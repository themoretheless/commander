# Archive group copy-count bounds — 2026-11-03

Independent adaptation of cohort #85 [fclones replica-count restrictions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pkolaczk/fclones/main/fclones/README.md) to retained archive groups. Group copies Min/Max are inclusive bounds on total members, including keepers. Blank means unbounded. Bounds combine with text/glob/regex/exact matching and existing group sort, without rescanning, altering scan results, applying keeper policies or changing selected IDs. Existing group action/report identities remain original scan ranks. Shown-group bulk actions and reports follow the combined view; global scan/keeper actions and full reports remain all-scanned scope.

Counts accept trimmed nonnegative ASCII decimal integers, at most 20 digits and no greater than the platform usize maximum. Invalid input or Min greater than Max shows no groups and disables shown-group actions/reports. A valid range with no matches is distinct from an error. Reset group search clears count bounds and matching options, retaining group sort. These bounds are runtime-only, reset on archive reopen; they restrict retained duplicate groups and do not discover unique entries.
