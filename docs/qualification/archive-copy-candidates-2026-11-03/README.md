# Restrict extra archive copies to matching candidates — 2026-11-03

Independent adaptation of cohort #85 [fclones name/path action restrictions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pkolaczk/fclones/main/fclones/src/config.rs). Copy candidates accepts newline-separated globs. Only a matching member may be selected as an extra copy; nonmatches are retained and count toward the minimum replica count. Protected members always remain keepers. Empty/blank-only candidate lists allow every member, preserving earlier behavior. Applying the policy removes noncandidate members from selection in applicable groups; groups skipped by the date restriction preserve their selection as before.

Filename only and Match case explicitly apply to both candidate and protection lists. Both lists share the existing bounded matcher and validation (16 nonempty patterns, 256 characters each, 8192 total characters, CRLF, empty lines ignored, literal spaces). Malformed candidate patterns disable both group/global actions and are refused before selection changes. Non-UTF-8 paths, stale identities and overlapping groups retain atomic refusal. Pending edits leave selection intact. Settings are runtime-only.
