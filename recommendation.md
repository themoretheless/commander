in [research.md](research.md): its `G001-G100` identifiers do not alter Track
F's exact count.

## Strategy

`master` is the mainline. The `refactor/god-removal-ui-state` branch is treated
as a **proven spike**, not a merge candidate: its ideas (Effect bus, `UiState`,
`Pane`/`CommandHandler` traits, tokio async) are re-landed onto `master` in
small, reviewable steps. It diverged on a stale base (`master` ~42 commits
ahead, the spike ~13), so a wholesale merge would be conflict-heavy and is
explicitly out of scope. Where a step below has a working reference on the
spike, that is noted as "mine from spike".

**Track A** decomposes the two god objects, while the D-track closes verified
correctness gaps in small passes. **Track B is now complete**; it remains below
as a code-verified capability record rather than a list of work to schedule.

risk. The keystone (A4) is deliberately not first: its payoff lives in
egui-frame focus behaviour the 424 tests cannot guard, so it must be verified by
hand in the running app.

## Track F - Top 500 review backlog (3 design/review iterations, 2026-07-09)

This is the requested 500-point pass. It is a compact, reviewable digest of the
first 500 entries in [backlog.md](backlog.md); the numbers are intentionally the
same as `backlog.md`, so the full original reasoning is one lookup away. Treat
[audit.md](audit.md) as the verified severity ranking, and Track F as the broad
map for steady cleanup, UX polish, performance work, and SOLID/DRY splitting.

Three sequential iterations were applied:

1. **Inventory iteration.** Pull the first 500 code-grounded findings from the
   621-item sweep and keep their original locations/categories.
2. **Designer + SOLID/DRY iteration.** Slice the list into ten reviewable
   chunks, separating domain logic, UI state, OS ports, rendering, and
   per-frame orchestration so each pass has one reason to change.
3. **Review/fix iteration.** Re-count exactly 500 items, check the docs against
   current code, and fix one critical issue immediately: D12 / audit #1, the
   `native_menu.rs` AppleScript literal injection in Get Info.
